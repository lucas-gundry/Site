About

I'm a security engineer focused on AI security and enterprise security: identity, detection, and running AI tools without handing them the keys.

How I work

  • Least privilege, tested. I check a role by what it refuses, not only by what it can do.
  • Code makes security decisions. AI is for summarizing, correlating, and explaining. It is never the only thing standing between a request and a consequential action.
  • A control isn't verified until the logs show it. When the admin console and the audit log disagree, I trust the log.
  • Measure before fixing. I write down what I think is wrong, build a way to check it, and keep the record even when I turn out to be wrong.
  • Secrets stay out of everything: repos, prompts, logs, screenshots, and this site.
